Certain sports have higher barriers to entry, thus reducing their talent pool. Types of barriers of entry include:
- Equipment costs. Examples include golf, ice hockey, rowing, sailing, auto racing.
- Location and weather constraints. Examples include rowing, ice hockey, auto racing, alpine skiing, and sailing.
- Team sports requiring teammates or opponents.
- Genetic requirements, such as body shape, size, strength. Examples include ballet, weightlifting, basketball.
- Cultural barriers, such as certain sports not being popular in a region.

For sports with high barriers to entry, it is hard to practice the sport without complex equipment, proper location, and other people. So, all things equal, sports with fewer barriers to entry tend to be more popular, have a larger talent pool, and be more competitive. Simple sports include most track and field events, and soccer. Sports with higher barriers to entry tend to be less popular, but are perceived as higher status.
